Key Findings

  • Manufacturing is the single largest vertical at 24% of deployments, driven by real-time defect detection and predictive maintenance.
  • On-device LLM inference (sub-7B parameter models) emerged as a breakout use-case in H2 2025, reducing cloud egress costs by up to 60%.
  • Data residency regulations in the EU and APAC are forcing multinationals to deploy regional edge nodes ahead of commercial demand signals.
  • Kubernetes-based orchestration (K3s, MicroShift) now covers 71% of managed edge deployments, up from 38% in 2023.
  • Security remains the top barrier to adoption, cited by 58% of CIOs in our survey of 400 enterprises.
